ISPR warns any future Indian "military misadventure" will draw a "disproportionate response" after Discovery docuseries on Operation Sindoor

FactsPakistan's Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR) said on August 18 that "any future military misadventure will be met with a firm, decisive and disproportionate response," reacting to the Discovery docuseries "Declassified: Operation Sindoor," in which senior Indian political and military leaders describe last year's 88-hour conflict as a "100 percent mission success." Global AI

Google, UK aviation bodies and universities launch Operation Blue Skies, a £5M state-backed trial to cut contrail warming over the North Atlantic

FactsGoogle, the UK Department for Transport, the Met Office, NATS, Contrails.org, Imperial College London and Cambridge University launched Operation Blue Skies on August 18, a £5 million (partly £2.65M DfT-funded), 30-month trial described as the world's first oceanic-airspace-scale contrail-avoidance program. Starting this winter, air traffic controllers will use Google's AI contrail forecasting to reroute a small share of the roughly 10,000 flights/year through NATS-controlled Shanwick oceanic airspace — responsible for about 5% of global contrail warming — by up to 2,000 feet, across trial windows in winter 2026-27 and 2027-28.

Ideal CaseThe trial demonstrates that AI-guided altitude changes meaningfully cut contrail-driven warming at a cost airlines and regulators can scale globally.

ImpactGlobal — the first government-backed test of AI-directed flight-path changes at the scale of an entire oceanic airspace region.

Global Military Development

US has lost roughly 45 MQ-9 Reaper drones — about a quarter of its fleet — in the Iran war, officials say

FactsThe US military has lost at least 45 MQ-9 Reaper drones, or about 25% of its pre-war fleet, during operations against Iran under what US forces have termed "Operation Epic Fury," according to three US officials cited by the Washington Post on August 13 and corroborated by subsequent reporting through August 18. The Reapers, costing $30-50 million each depending on sensors and weapons, were used heavily for surveillance and strikes around the Strait of Hormuz, with the losses representing a potential taxpayer cost exceeding $1.3 billion.

Ideal CaseThe Pentagon absorbs the attrition through accelerated replacement production without degrading ongoing ISR coverage of the Strait of Hormuz.

ImpactGlobal — a historic rate of drone attrition that will shape US Air Force procurement and unmanned-systems doctrine going forward.
