AI's $2 Trillion Week: Apple Dethrones Nvidia as India's Defence Spend Tops $71B and NATO's Drone Pledge Anchors the West

Editors' Desk — synthesised from six daily T Digest editions, July 20–25, 2026 (Sunday's edition unavailable)

India

Political

NEET-leak fallout escalated all week — from Monsoon Session's opening tension through Delhi's tear-gas clashes and the brief detention of Rahul Gandhi, Priyanka Gandhi and Akhilesh Yadav — ending in a Maharashtra Bandh and Modi's fast-track-courts pledge, which protesters rejected as insufficient.

Sources: Deccan Herald, LatestLY, Al Jazeera, India TV News
Military

Modernisation hit a record pace: HAL's ₹13,500cr Su-30MKI buy and INS Malvan's commissioning opened the week, DRDO charted "Stealth Beyond 2030" and Bharat Forge unveiled new FICVs, before a record $25B air-defence clearance pushed India's FY26 DAC approvals past $71B.

Sources: Indian Defence News, Adda247, ANI, Defense News
Economy

Equities slid all week as Brent crude climbed from $92 to above $100 on Mideast tensions, dragging the Sensex from 77,470 toward 76,391 and the rupee to 96.6/dollar, even as May exports hit a six-month high of $45.2B and RBI flagged inflation risk early on.

Sources: Business Standard, India TV News, Trading Economics, Goodreturns
Technology

Semicon 2.0 anchored coverage — Cabinet cleared the ₹1.27 lakh crore push and the Vivo-Dixon smartphone venture advanced — while Tata Electronics pragmatically scaled its Dholera fab back to a mature 90nm node rather than the originally planned 28nm.

Sources: Inc42, TechCrunch, DIGITIMES, Business Standard

Pakistan

Political

The AJK election dominated the week, building from PTI-boycott friction and deadly JAC unrest through Bilawal's Muzaffarabad rally and reconciliation-commission proposal to a mass PPP show-of-force at Lahore's Minar-e-Pakistan, days ahead of the July 27 vote.

Sources: The Nation, Geo News, Al Jazeera, Islamabad Post
Military

Counter-militancy operations widened on two fronts: the Bannu campaign held near 24 killed while Balochistan's Operation Shaban surged past 129 dead since July 5, even as Munir's Ankara visit deepened Turkish defence ties and Karachi Shipyard advanced its Jinnah-class frigate.

Sources: Defence.pk/ISPR, Pakistan Today, Organiser, Quwa
Economy

The KSE-100 peaked near a record before Middle East-driven selling pulled it from 174,551 to 171,021 by week's end, even as S&P upgraded Pakistan's sovereign rating to 'B' and the IMF trimmed its FY27 growth forecast to 3.5%.

Sources: Business Recorder, The Nation, 24NewsHD, S&P Global
Technology

A record $4.6B FY26 tech-export figure (+21% YoY) anchored the week's coverage, capped by Shehbaz Sharif and Munir inaugurating "Karakoram One" — Pakistan's largest AI data centre — in Islamabad on July 24.

Sources: ProPakistani, Radio Pakistan, Dawn.com, Express Tribune

Global — AI, Military Development & UAS / Ctr-UAS

Global AI

A week of capital escalation: Apple overtook Nvidia as the world's most valuable company amid a $2 trillion AI-chip rally, Microsoft lifted 2026 capex to $190B and Seoul pledged $880B, while China's 29-nation WAICO bloc launched as UN dialogues kept warning of "catastrophic harm."

Sources: CNBC/Bloomberg, Tech Startups, UN News, AI Weekly
Global Military Development

Sixth-gen and naval rearmament advanced together — GCAP's £4.6B Edgewing contract, Poland's first Cameri-built F-35A, and Germany's €6.3B frigate buy — while Ukraine's F-16 fleet scored its first confirmed air-to-air kill, later verified to the Senate by the Joint Chiefs chairman.

Sources: FlightGlobal, The Aviationist, Naval News, Defense News
Global UAS / Ctr-UAS

NATO's $40B counter-drone pledge through 2031 anchored coverage all week alongside AeroVironment's recurring $500M Army contract, as the Pentagon opened a dedicated Unmanned Systems Office and finalised SAFER SKIES rules to fast-track counter-drone technology.

Sources: NATO.int, Army Recognition, The Drone Girl, DroneLife
